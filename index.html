Why MOMNTUM exists

Home training shouldn't need to be complicated.

MOMNTUM started with a simple frustration: getting fitter at home shouldn't mean expensive memberships, cluttered apps or another monthly subscription.

01 — The beginning

It wasn't supposed to become an app.

The original idea was simply to create a useful collection of structured calisthenics workouts that could be followed at home.

That idea became a PDF, then an interactive PowerPoint, then a browser-based training tool. Eventually, almost organically, it grew into MOMNTUM.

02 — Built through iteration

Hundreds of hours. Dozens of iterations.

Almost every part of the experience has been designed, tested, questioned and rebuilt — workout generation, equipment-aware training, timers, progression, onboarding, visual design, sound and a bespoke exercise animation system.

MOMNTUM wasn't conceived as a finished software product. It became one through iteration: keeping what worked, rebuilding what didn't and removing things that didn't earn their place.

03 — Ownership

Fitness shouldn't be rented forever.

Fitness apps increasingly turn exercise into a service: accounts, adverts, premium tiers, locked features and subscriptions that continue for as long as you want access.

Buy it once. Own it. Train with it.

MOMNTUM was created as an alternative. No advertising. No recurring subscription. No artificial feature restrictions designed to push an upgrade.

04 — The product ethos

Every feature has to justify its place.

MOMNTUM isn't trying to become a social network, a lifestyle platform or a replacement for a personal trainer. It doesn't need feeds, leaderboards or endless metrics.

Does this make it easier to start a workout, complete it and come back again?

That question has shaped the app. Keep the interface focused. Keep decisions simple. Use equipment if you have it; train without it if you don't. Provide enough guidance to keep someone moving without burying them beneath information.

05 — Knowing what to leave out

More features don't automatically make a better app.

Features have been added, removed, redesigned and sometimes deliberately parked when they didn't improve the core experience.

MOMNTUM hasn't been built by continually adding more. Much of its identity has come from deciding what it doesn't need to be.

From a personal workout guide to an independent fitness app.

The technology changed. The ambition grew. The original idea didn't.

Make home training simple enough to start, useful enough to stick with, and something you can actually own.
